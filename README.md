# Business & Advanced Prompt Engineering Collection

A production-ready repository of structured business prompts, execution frameworks, and citation-focused research protocols. This collection provides actionable frameworks designed to eliminate AI hallucination, enforce strict operational and budgetary constraints, and deliver audit-ready decision outputs across multiple business functions[cite: 1, 2, 3, 4].

## 📋 Core Modules & Modules Summary

### 1. Foundational Prompting (`/foundational`)
Focuses on converting vague input requests into structured, actionable business deliverables using the **Role, Context, Task, Format (RCTF)** framework[cite: 4]:
* **Vagueness Correction:** Transforming generic prompts into budget- and channel-bound execution plans[cite: 4].
* **Prompt Comparison:** Structural comparison between open-ended queries and constrained role-based prompts[cite: 4].
* **Standardized Templates:** Business context frameworks for e-commerce retention, local marketing, and financial concept explanations[cite: 4].

### 2. Advanced Prompt Engineering (`/advanced`)
Utilizes structured output constraints, multi-stage prompt chaining, and iterative refinement[cite: 2]:
* **Structured Multi-Constraint Frameworks:** Hard ceilings on budgets (e.g., ≤ ₹20,000), channel limits (Instagram & WhatsApp only), and strict formatting[cite: 2].
* **Prompt Chaining Sequences:**
  1. *Segment Identification* $\rightarrow$ 2. *Segment Selection & Rationale* $\rightarrow$ 3. *Strategy Design* $\rightarrow$ 4. *Campaign Calendar* $\rightarrow$ 5. *Executive Summary*[cite: 2].
* **Iterative Refinement Examples:** Before-and-after transformations for emails, presentation decks, job descriptions, and waiting-time optimizations[cite: 2].

### 3. Business Prompts & Decision Support (`/business`)
Tailored workflows for core business operations[cite: 1]:
* **Marketing:** Post-purchase retention loops, student-targeted café campaigns, organic product launch calendars, and customer segmentation[cite: 1].
* **Human Resources (HR):** Non-biased interview design, structured 5-day onboarding/training schedules, employee motivation diagnostics, and objective performance evaluations[cite: 1].
* **Finance:** Small business profit-and-loss calculations, current ratio liquidity analysis, expense review frameworks, and cost-reduction audits[cite: 1].
* **Operations:** Deliveries, wrong-item and refund process diagnostics, inventory management (ABC classification), and 30-day operational action plans[cite: 1].

### 4. AI Research, Verification & Citations (`/research-citations`)
Strict protocols designed to prevent hallucinated data and enforce empirical accountability:
* **Fact vs. Assumption vs. Recommendation:** Explicit separation of verified facts from unverified operational assumptions[cite: 1, 3].
* **Hallucination Detection:** Systematic checklists to evaluate fake DOIs, suspicious journal titles, generic literature, and unverified statistical claims[cite: 3].
* **Evidence Triangulation:** Verification pipelines combining primary enterprise surveys, academic studies, and verified platform disclosures[cite: 3].
* **Evidence Matrices:** Structural templates requiring proof of verification before any data is admitted into an executive report[cite: 3].
